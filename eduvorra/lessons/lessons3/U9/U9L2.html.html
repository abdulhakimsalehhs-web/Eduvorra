📚 Relative Pronouns
الضمائر الوصلية — تُضاف لتعطي معلومات إضافية عن الاسم

What is a Relative Clause? — ما هي الجملة الوصلية؟

Relative clauses add information about a noun in the main clause.

الجملة الوصلية تُضيف معلومات عن اسم في الجملة الرئيسية.

Which pronoun to use? — أي ضمير نستخدم؟

WHO / THAT
→ For People (أشخاص)
THAT / WHICH
→ For Things & Animals (أشياء وحيوانات)
💡 Examples — Relative Pronouns
أمثلة — الضمائر الوصلية

FOR PEOPLE (أشخاص) — use who or that

Without relative clause
The man was talking to the tourists. He was friendly.
With relative clause
The man who/that was talking to the tourists was friendly.

FOR THINGS & ANIMALS (أشياء/حيوانات) — use that or which

Without relative clause
The horse pulled the carriage. It was black.
With relative clause
The horse that/which pulled the carriage was black.
⏳ Past Progressive with When & While
الماضي المستمر مع when و while

Formation — التكوين

was / were + verb-ing

نستخدم was مع المفرد وwere مع الجمع.

Use 1 — Event happening WHEN another event interrupted it

حدث كان يقع عندما قاطعه حدث آخر

I was looking in the store window when the thief grabbed my bag.

Use 2 — Two actions happening at the SAME TIME

حدثان وقعا في نفس الوقت

He was driving while he was talking on his cell phone.

📋 Summary — ملخص الدرس
Grammar PointRuleExample
who / that For people (أشخاص) The man who helped me was nice.
that / which For things & animals The bag that I lost was blue.
Past Prog. + when Ongoing action + interruption I was sleeping when he called.
Past Prog. + while Two simultaneous actions She was cooking while he was reading.

🌟 Key Tip — نصيحة مهمة

Use when → one long action + one short interrupting action.
Use while → two long actions happening at the same time.
استخدم when عندما تنقطع الحالة بحدث مفاجئ، وwhile عندما يتزامن حدثان.
