Listen & Discuss — Some Vacation Favorites
🌍 GLOBAL TOURS — Vacation for You!
We take you anywhere in the world. What are you going to do on your vacation? Are you going to travel around or just relax in one place? You're going to have the time of your life!
📐
Grammar Focus — "Going to"
📌 Structure (التركيب)
Affirmative: Subject + am / is / are + going to + base verb
Negative: Subject + am / is / are + not + going to + base verb
Question: Am / Is / Are + Subject + going to + base verb + ?
💡 Examples from the text
You're going to see some of the most awesome marine life!
You're going to experience the old and the new in Dubai.
You're going to visit a desert oasis in Oman.
You're going to come into contact with thousands of plant species.
نستخدم "going to" للحديث عن خطط المستقبل أو التنبؤات المبنية على دليل واضح.

🔍
Quick Check B — Comprehension Questions
Question 1
What does Global Tours offer?
✅ ANSWER Global Tours offers vacations to different places around the world — including scuba diving, cultural trips, trekking, safaris, desert tours, and ecotourism.
Question 2
What can you do in the Red Sea?
✅ ANSWER You can go scuba diving and see the peaceful coral reefs, colorful marine life, sea turtles, lionfish, manta rays, and dolphins.
Question 3
What are visitors going to see in Petra?
✅ ANSWER Visitors are going to see the magnificent archaeological site, wonderful buildings, and tombs carved out in the rock.
Question 4
What kind of vacation are you going to have in Malaysia?
✅ ANSWER You are going to have an ecotourism vacation — hiking through the rainforest at Kinabalu Park and learning about unique ecosystems, plants, and animals.
