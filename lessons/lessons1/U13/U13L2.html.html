🔀 Exercise A — Unscramble the Sentences

Rearrange the words to make correct sentences. Click 👁️ to check your answer.

1. She / going / a new dress / is / to buy
She is going to buy a new dress.
2. jeans / are / to the park / They / to wear / going
They are going to wear jeans to the park.
3. a new pair of sneakers / to shop for / going / I'm
I'm going to shop for a new pair of sneakers.
4. to wear / sandals / is / to the beach / He / going
He is going to wear sandals to the beach.
5. are / for Dad / We / going / a tie / to buy
We are going to buy a tie for Dad.
6. to need / going / for work / are / a suit / You
You are going to need a suit for work.
💬 Exercise B — Ask and Answer

Model Dialogue:

A: What do you usually wear on a plane?
B: I usually wear a T-shirt and jeans.

Now ask and answer for each place. Click 👁️ for a suggested answer.

1. What do you usually wear at home?
I usually wear comfortable clothes / pajamas at home.
2. What do you usually wear to school?
I usually wear my school uniform to school.
3. What do you usually wear to a football game?
I usually wear jeans and a T-shirt / a sports jersey to a football game.
4. What do you usually wear to the beach?
I usually wear light clothes and sandals to the beach.
5. What do you usually wear in cold weather?
I usually wear a coat and warm clothes in cold weather.
6. What do you usually wear in hot weather?
I usually wear light and loose clothes in hot weather.
7. Your idea! What do YOU wear to a special place?

📌 Summary / ملخص الدرس
✅
Affirmative
Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb
She's going to wear jeans.
❌
Negative
Subject + am not / isn't / aren't + going to + verb
He isn't going to wear shorts.
❓
Question
Am/Is/Are + subject + going to + verb?
Are you going to wear a suit?
⚡
Short Answers
Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
