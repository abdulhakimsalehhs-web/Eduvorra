📝 Grammar in the Email

How does Badria talk about current and future activities?

كيف تصف بدرية الأنشطة الحالية والمستقبلية؟

🔵 Present Continuous — Current Activities

المضارع المستمر — الأنشطة التي تحدث الآن

🔵 We are having a fantastic winter vacation in Abha!
🔵 Today we are visiting the Shadda Palace Museum and the Abha Great Mosque.
🟠 "Going to" — Future Plans

المضارع المستمر للمستقبل — خطط مستقبلية

🟠 Tomorrow we are going to the Asir National Park and we are going hiking in the forest there.
✏️ Use Present Continuous for NOW and going to / future plans for TOMORROW or later. Use BOTH in your email!

✍️ Exercise C — Write an Email

Imagine you are at a resort in Saudi Arabia. Write an email to a friend. Say what you can do there and what you are planning to do during your stay. Use your notes from the chart.

تخيل أنك في منتجع بالمملكة العربية السعودية. اكتب بريداً إلكترونياً لصديق. أخبره بما يمكنك فعله وما تخطط للقيام به.

💡 Email Structure (Follow This!)
1️⃣
Greeting: Dear [friend's name],
2️⃣
Opening: Where you are + general feeling (Present Continuous)
3️⃣
Activities: What you can do there (can + verb)
4️⃣
Today's plan: What you are doing today (Present Continuous)
5️⃣
Tomorrow's plan: What you are going to do (going to)
6️⃣
Closing: See you soon, [your name]
📧 Model Email — Al-Ula
To:sara@supergoal.com
From:student@supergoal.com
Subject:My Vacation!

Dear Sara,

My family and I are having an amazing vacation in Al-Ula! There are so many things to do here. You can go hiking in the rocky mountains, ride a camel, and visit the ancient Hegra ruins. At night, you can enjoy stargazing in the desert.

Today we are visiting the Hegra archaeological site and taking a hot-air balloon ride over the rock formations.

Tomorrow we are going to the old town of Al-Ula and we are going to buy beautiful souvenirs and try local food.

I love the stunning scenery and the ancient history here. You must visit one day!

See you soon,
Your Name

  • Use Present Continuous: "We are visiting...", "We are having..."
  • Use going to: "We are going to...", "We are going to buy..."
  • Use can for general activities: "You can go hiking..."
  • Start with Dear [Name], and end with See you soon,
  • Mention today and tomorrow plans!

🏆 Challenge Question

Think harder! Can you answer this?

فكّر أكثر! هل يمكنك الإجابة؟

🌟 Challenge

Badria writes: "Tomorrow we are going to the Asir National Park and we are going hiking in the forest there."

Can you rewrite this sentence using two SEPARATE future sentences? One with "going to" and one with Present Continuous for future.

Sentence 1 (going to):
Tomorrow, we are going to visit the Asir National Park.

Sentence 2 (Present Continuous for future):
We are hiking in the forest there tomorrow.

✅ Both sentences express future plans — "going to" is more formal, while Present Continuous feels more immediate and personal!
