📖 Explanation

What is a Gerund? verb + ing

A gerund is the -ing form of a verb.
It acts like a noun and answers the question what.
الجاردة هي الفعل المضاف إليه ing — تعمل كاسم وتجيب على سؤال "ماذا؟"

🔗 Verbs followed by a GERUND: (أفعال يتبعها الجاردة)

They spend their free time playing basketball.
يقضون وقت فراغهم في لعب كرة السلة.
He enjoys skateboarding in the park.
يستمتع بركوب السكيت في الحديقة.
She can't stand waiting in long lines.
لا تتحمل الانتظار في الطوابير الطويلة.
📖 Explanation

What is an Infinitive? to + verb

An infinitive is to + the base form of a verb.
Like gerunds, infinitives act like nouns and answer the question what.
المصدر هو to + الفعل الأساسي — يعمل أيضاً كاسم ويجيب على سؤال "ماذا؟"

🔗 Verbs followed by an INFINITIVE: (أفعال يتبعها المصدر)

I like to watch TV.
أحب مشاهدة التلفزيون.
She wants to travel abroad.
تريد السفر إلى الخارج.
⚠️ like, love, hate, and prefer can go with either a gerund or an infinitive.
بعض الأفعال مثل like و love و hate و prefer تقبل الجاردة والمصدر معاً
🔀 Compare

Gerund vs. Infinitive

🏃
GERUND
verb + -ing
enjoy swimming
dislike cooking
can't stand waiting
💡
INFINITIVE
to + verb
want to go
would like to eat
hate to wake up early

There are lots of fun activities to do at Beachside. My dad and brothers enjoy (2. do) water sports. They usually rent a boat. My dad and Ahmed spend the day (3. fish) . 📋 Summary

What did we learn?

🏃

Gerund = verb + -ing

Acts like a noun. Used after: enjoy, dislike, hate, like, love, prefer, can't stand, feel like, spend time.
يعمل كاسم. يأتي بعد: enjoy, dislike, hate, like, love, prefer...

💡

Infinitive = to + base verb

Acts like a noun. Used after: want, would like, love, hate, like, prefer.
يعمل كاسم. يأتي بعد: want, would like, love, hate...

⚡

Some verbs take BOTH

like, love, hate, prefer → can use gerund OR infinitive.
بعض الأفعال تقبل الجاردة والمصدر معاً.

📝

Remember the key verbs!

Gerund only: enjoy, can't stand, feel like, spend time, dislike.
Infinitive only: want, would like.
احفظ الأفعال المهمة.
