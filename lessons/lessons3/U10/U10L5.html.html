📖 Grammar: Relative Clauses – الجمل الوصفية

📝 Writing Corner – الزاوية الكتابية

We use relative clauses to make clear which person or thing we are talking about.
نستخدم الجمل الوصفية لتوضيح الشخص أو الشيء الذي نتحدث عنه

1️⃣ Use who or that for people – للأشخاص
I can't stand people who/that cut in front of the line.
لا أطيق الأشخاص الذين يقتحمون الطابور
2️⃣ Use which or that for things – للأشياء
It's annoying when salespeople try to sell you things which/that you don't need.
من المزعج أن يحاول البائعون بيعك أشياء لا تحتاجها
3️⃣ Use whose to show possession – للملكية
I dislike people whose behavior is rude.
لا أحب الأشخاص الذين سلوكهم وقح
4️⃣ Use where for places – للأماكن
It annoys me when people park their cars on sidewalks where pedestrians walk.
يزعجني أن يوقف الناس سياراتهم على الأرصفة التي يمشي فيها المشاة
Relative Pronoun Used For Arabic
who / that People للأشخاص
which / that Things للأشياء
whose Possession للملكية
where Places للأماكن

💡 Tips for Writing – نصائح للكتابة

  • Use relative clauses: who, that, which, whose, where
  • Express your feelings: It annoys me when… / I can't stand people who…
  • Describe the effect: This makes others feel… / It causes…

✅ Model Forum Entries – Exercise C

  1. It really bothers me when people who are on public transport talk loudly on their phones. This is very inconsiderate and disturbs everyone around them.
  2. I can't stand people whose dogs run free in parks where children play. It makes parents feel unsafe about letting their children enjoy the park.
  3. One thing that really annoys me is when drivers park in places where they block traffic. This causes long delays for everyone.
  4. I hate advertisements that pop up on my screen while I'm working. They interrupt my concentration and slow my computer down.
  5. It gets on my nerves when people say things that aren't true just to get attention. This destroys trust between people and makes communication difficult.
