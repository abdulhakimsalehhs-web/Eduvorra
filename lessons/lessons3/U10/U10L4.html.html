💬 Reading Text — "Complaints" Forum
📋 What Can't You Stand? What Drives You Crazy?

Below are real complaints shared on a forum. Read each one carefully.

Standing in line at checkout counters at the supermarket.
Listening to people talking on their cell phones in restaurants. It's annoying.
When people talk on their cell phones and drive.
People who use words like cool and stuff all the time. Also, people who use the word like all the time in conversation. The word takes the place of adjectives, nouns, and intelligence.
Rushing to the phone and finding out it's a telemarketer who's calling.
People who lost weight and now try to control what others eat.
People who are always late, and people who are late and don't apologize.
When salespeople try to talk you into buying something you don't want or need.
People who don't say "please" and "thank you."
When people enter a door or an elevator and don't hold it open for the person who is right behind them.
I can't stand people who chew with their mouths open.
It drives me insane when I call up a company and I get "Press 1 for A, 2 for B, 3 for C," and so on. Or the common assurance "Your call will be answered in the order it was received," and then you have to wait forever while they put you on hold.
When people don't give up their subway or bus seats to seniors or to those with disabilities.
People who stand in the way of others who want to use an escalator or doorway.
People who change the TV channel without asking when someone else is clearly watching.
People who stand in someone else's line of sight in front of the TV during the last 30 seconds of any show or game.
People who "borrow" stuff without asking and don't give it back.
When someone constantly interrupts you while you're speaking.
When people leave a pile of dirty dishes in the kitchen for others to clean up.

✏️ Guided Examples — Phrasal Verbs in Context
1️⃣ Using "let out"

The forum says: "Do you have a complaint? Let it out. Here's your forum."

"Let it out" = express yourself freely (تعبّر عن نفسك بحرية). It means stop holding your feelings inside and share them openly.
2️⃣ Using "find out"

"Rushing to the phone and finding out it's a telemarketer who's calling."

"Find out" = to learn / discover (يكتشف). Here it means the person discovered the caller was a telemarketer — very annoying!
3️⃣ Using "give up"

"When people don't give up their subway or bus seats to seniors."

"Give up" = let someone have something (يتنازل). Here it means letting an elderly person or disabled person have your seat.
4️⃣ Using "clean up"

"When people leave a pile of dirty dishes in the kitchen for others to clean up."

"Clean up" = make things neat / tidy (يرتّب، ينظّف). It means washing dishes and making the kitchen clean.
📋 All Example Meanings (Summary)
let out → express yourself freely
find out → learn / discover
give up → let someone have something
clean up → make things neat

📌 Exercise C — Answer True or False about the complaints forum

In general, people disapprove of the following:

1.
People talking on cell phones in public places
TRUE — The forum says listening to people on cell phones in restaurants is annoying, and driving while using a phone is also mentioned.
2.
People on a diet
FALSE — People don't complain about those who are on a diet. The complaint is about people who lost weight and now try to control what others eat, not people who are simply on a diet.
3.
People who don't wait their turn
TRUE — The forum mentions "Standing in line at checkout counters" and the frustration of waiting (like being put on hold on the phone).
4.
People who do the dishes right after a meal
FALSE — The complaint is about people who leave dirty dishes, NOT about people who clean up right away. Cleaning up right away is good behavior!
5.
People using an escalator instead of the stairs
FALSE — The complaint is about people who stand in the way of others on an escalator or doorway, NOT about using the escalator itself.

💡 Key Language Points
💡 Phrasal verbs are two-word verbs. The second word changes the meaning completely. Example: give vs give up vs give back.
💡 The text uses informal language like cool, stuff, and like — these are common in everyday speech but not in formal writing.
💡 A forum is an online space where people share opinions and ideas freely. The complaints forum is a great example of authentic English.
💡 To talk about things that annoy you, use: "I can't stand…" or "It drives me crazy when…"

🔑 Model Answer:

The writer humorously suggests that using vague words like cool, stuff, and like replaces real thinking and vocabulary — it shows a lack of intelligence because the speaker isn't choosing precise words.

Example complaint sentences:
• "I can't stand people who chew with their mouths open in restaurants."
• "It drives me insane when someone changes the TV channel while I'm watching!"
• "I can't stand people who are always late and never apologize."
