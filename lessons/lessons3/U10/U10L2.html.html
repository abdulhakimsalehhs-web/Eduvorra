📗 Present Perfect with Already, Yet, Just
المضارع التام مع already و yet و just

We use the Present Perfect to talk about events that happened before now. The event may have happened earlier than expected.

نستخدم المضارع التام للحديث عن أحداث وقعت قبل الآن، وقد تكون حدثت أبكر مما هو متوقع.
Questions ❓ Affirmative ✅ Negative ❌
Have you done the dishes yet? Yes, I've already done them. No, I haven't done them yet.
Have you already washed the clothes? Yes, I've just washed them. No, I haven't washed them yet.
💡 Key Rule
  • Already → used in ✅ affirmative sentences (الإيجاب)
  • Yet → used in ❓ questions and ❌ negatives (الأسئلة والنفي)
  • Just → means "very recently" (للتو / منذ قليل)
📘 Verb + Gerund
الفعل + الاسم الفعلي (ing)

Use the gerund form (verb + -ing) after these verbs:

avoid enjoy give up imagine mind stop can't stand finish hate keep miss suggest
هذه الأفعال تتبعها دائماً صيغة (فعل + ing) وليس المصدر
📝 Examples
He can't stand waiting in line.
هو لا يطيق الانتظار في الطابور.
I don't enjoy sitting in the sun.
لا أستمتع بالجلوس في الشمس.
We miss being with our friends.
نحن نفتقد كوننا مع أصدقائنا.
They stop playing tennis in the winter.
يتوقفون عن لعب التنس في الشتاء.
📙 Two-Word Verbs (Phrasal Verbs)
الأفعال الثنائية (Phrasal Verbs)

Two-word verbs are common in English. They have a verb + particle.
Object pronouns always come between the verb and the particle.

الأفعال الثنائية تتكون من فعل + جسيم. الضمير دائماً يأتي بين الفعل والجسيم.
Full Noun Noun Separated Pronoun
Turn down the TV. Turn the TV down. Turn it down.
Put away your clothes. Put your clothes away. Put them away.
⚠️ Rule: When using a pronoun (it, them, him, her…), it MUST go between the verb and particle. You cannot say: "Turn down it" ❌ → Correct: "Turn it down" ✅

📋 Summary
ملخص الدرس
1
Present Perfect with Already / Yet / Just
Use already in affirmatives, yet in questions & negatives, just for very recent actions.
already للإيجاب • yet للسؤال والنفي • just لما وقع حديثاً
2
Verb + Gerund (-ing)
Verbs like enjoy, hate, avoid, finish, miss, can't stand are always followed by a gerund.
بعض الأفعال تتبعها دائماً صيغة (فعل + ing)
3
Phrasal Verbs
Object pronouns go BETWEEN the verb and particle: Turn it off ✅ / Turn off it ❌
الضمير يأتي دائماً بين الفعل والجسيم
🌟 Key Sentences to Remember:
• I've already done the dishes.
• Have you cleaned your room yet?
• She enjoys cooking for her family.
• Put them away.
