In this lesson, you will learn how to use the modal verbs can, may, and could to ask for permission, agree, and refuse politely — and how to complete real-life telephone conversations.

🎯 Objectives
What You Will Learn
  • ✅ Use can / may / could to ask for permission politely.
  • ✅ Use appropriate responses to agree or refuse a request.
  • ✅ Use would like (to) for polite requests.
  • ✅ Complete telephone conversations using modal verbs correctly.
  • ✅ Practice real-life scenarios: banks, hotels, taxis, and medical clinics.

سنتعلم اليوم كيف نستخدم الأفعال الشكلية (can / may / could) لطلب الإذن والموافقة والرفض بأسلوب مؤدب في محادثات الهاتف.

📚 Grammar
Can / May / Could

We use the modal verbs can, may, and could to ask for permission.

Ask for Permission Agree ✅ Refuse ❌
Can I ask you a question? Yes, of course. I'm sorry, but…
May I speak to the manager? Certainly. / Sure. That's not possible…
Could I make a reservation? All right. I'm afraid…
FYI: May and Could are more formal than Can.
May و Could أكثر رسمية من Can
💬 Would like (to) — Polite Requests

We use would like (to)… for polite requests:

I would like to book a room.
أود حجز غرفة.

💡 Examples
Modal Verbs in Action
🏦 At a Bank
Can I ask you a question? — Yes, of course.
May I speak to the manager? — I'm sorry, he's busy right now.
🏨 At a Hotel
Could I make a reservation? — Certainly. For how many nights?
I would like to book a room for May 14th. — All right.
🚕 Ordering a Taxi
Can we help you? — I would like a taxi to the airport, please.
🏥 Medical Clinic
Could I make an appointment to see the doctor?
I'm afraid that we don't have any available appointments on Tuesday.

📌 Summary
What We Learned Today

🔵 Can

Used to ask for permission informally.
"Can I ask you a question?"

🟢 May / Could

More formal permission requests.
"May I speak to the manager?"

✅ Agreeing

"Yes, of course." / "Certainly." / "All right." / "Sure."

❌ Refusing

"I'm sorry, but…" / "I'm afraid…" / "That's not possible."

💜 Would like (to)

Polite request form.
"I would like to book a room."

📞 Telephone Skills

Bank • Hotel • Taxi • Medical Clinic conversations.
