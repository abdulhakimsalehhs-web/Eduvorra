📖 Exercise 9A — Read the Email
📌 Task: Read the email. Circle the adjectives and underline the adverbs.
From: khalid_2001@sgmail.com
Subject: Greek island
Hi Saeed,
I hope you're well. I'm writing from the Greek island of Paros. You know how I always wanted to visit Greece. Well, I'm finally here. It's a dream come true!
First, we flew to Athens. We spent a wonderful day there visiting the Parthenon and the Acropolis Museum. Amazing! The next day, we went to the port of Piraeus and caught a high-speed ferry to the island. The voyage took less than 3 hours.
We're staying in a comfortable hotel in the capital, Parikia. It's a very picturesque town, with its narrow streets and traditional white houses that have blue doors and windows. In the evening, the stores, cafés, and restaurants are crowded with tourists. It's so lively, and I have to say Greek food is absolutely delicious!
Of course, the island has many scenic beaches with crystal clear waters and soft sand. Tomorrow, we're going to spend the day at Golden Beach. I'm really excited because I'm going to try windsurfing! Apart from the spectacular beaches, we plan to explore the unique sights of the island. We're going to visit the Archaeological Museum and the Valley of Butterflies.
I'll tell you more about my adventures in Paros soon. Write me back with your news.
Best wishes,
Khalid

🏗️ How to Write a Friendly Email
كيف تكتب رسالة بريد إلكتروني لصديق؟ Follow this structure every time!
1

Greetings (التحية)

Start with a friendly greeting. Address your friend by name.

ابدأ بتحية ودية مثل: Hi [Name], / Dear [Name],
2

Describe the Journey (وصف الرحلة)

Explain how you got there — flight, ferry, car? How long did it take?

كيف وصلت؟ ركبت طائرة؟ سفينة؟ وكم استغرق الوصول؟
3

Describe the Place (وصف المكان)

Use adjectives! Talk about the town, hotel, streets, food, people.

استخدم صفات وصفية جذابة لوصف المكان والطعام والناس.
4

Activities You Plan to Do (الأنشطة المخططة)

Use "going to" to talk about planned activities.

استخدم "going to" للحديث عن الأنشطة المستقبلية المخططة.
5

Closing (الختام)

End warmly. Promise to write again. Use: Best wishes / Take care / Yours

اختم برسالة ودية وعد بالكتابة مجددًا.

🗺️ Picnic Planning Guide
📍

Choose a Location

Where will your picnic be? A park, beach, garden, or mountain?

أين ستكون النزهة؟ حديقة، شاطئ، حديقة عامة؟
🍔

Plan the Food & Drinks

What food and drinks will you bring? Use adjectives: fresh, delicious, cold...

ماذا ستأخذون من طعام وشراب؟ استخدم صفات وصفية.
🎯

Plan the Activities

What are you going to do? Use "going to": We're going to play football / fly kites...

استخدم "going to" لذكر الأنشطة المخططة.
🖼️

Present to the Class

Use pictures and brochures to make your presentation exciting and visual.

استخدم الصور والكتيبات لتقديم عرض جذاب ومميز.
✅ Example Presentation Opener
Example opening for your presentation:

"Hello everyone! Our group is planning a picnic at Al-Urubah Park in Riyadh. We're going to have a wonderful time there! We're going to bring fresh sandwiches, cold juice, and delicious dates. We're going to play volleyball and fly kites. Here are some pictures of the park to show you how beautiful it is!"
💡 Model Examples — Adjectives & Adverbs in Context

🔸 Example 1: wonderful — Is it an adjective or adverb?

✅ Adjective — "We spent a wonderful day there." → It describes the noun day.

🔸 Example 2: absolutely — Is it an adjective or adverb?

✅ Adverb — "Greek food is absolutely delicious!" → It modifies the adjective delicious.

🔸 Example 3: finally — Is it an adjective or adverb?

✅ Adverb — "I'm finally here." → It modifies the verb am (here).

🔸 Example 4: picturesque — Is it an adjective or adverb?

✅ Adjective — "It's a very picturesque town." → It describes the noun town.

🔸 Example 5: scenic — Use it in a sentence.

✅ "The island has many scenic beaches with crystal clear waters." — Adjective describing beaches.
📋 Lesson Summary
🔵

Adjectives (صفات)

Describe nouns. Found in the email: wonderful, scenic, picturesque, comfortable, spectacular...

🟢

Adverbs (ظروف)

Modify verbs, adjectives, or adverbs. Found in the email: absolutely, finally, really, so...

✉️

Email Structure

Greeting → Journey → Place → Activities (going to) → Closing

📅

"Going to" for Plans

Use "be going to + verb" for future plans: We're going to visit the museum.

🗺️

Planning Chart

Always plan before you write! Use the chart to organize your email paragraphs.

🎒

Project

Plan a picnic as a group and present it using pictures and brochures.
