🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

✅Use be going to to talk about future plans (affirmative, negative, questions).
✅Form Yes/No questions and give short answers with be going to.
✅Form information (Wh-) questions using question words with be going to.
✅Understand the position of adjectives (before nouns / after the verb be).
✅Complete exercises and write sentences about vacation plans.

🌟 سنتعلم اليوم كيف نتحدث عن المستقبل باستخدام be going to، وكيف نضع الصفات في الجملة الإنجليزية.

📖 Future with Be Going To

Use be going to for the future, to talk about plans.
استخدم be going to للحديث عن خطط المستقبل.

TypeStructureExample
Affirmative (+) Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb I'm going to travel to Jordan.
Negative (–) Subject + am/is/are not + going to + verb I'm not going to visit Petra.
Yes/No Question (?) Am/Is/Are + subject + going to + verb? Are you going to travel to Jordan?
Short Answer (+) Yes, I am.
Short Answer (–) No, I'm not.

📌 ملاحظة: نستخدم am مع I، وis مع he/she/it، وare مع you/we/they.

❓ Information Questions
What are you going to do on your vacation?I'm going to travel to Africa.
When is he going to leave?He's going to leave next week.
Which countries is he going to visit?He's going to visit Tunisia and Morocco.
How are we going to go?We're going to go by plane.
Where am I going to stay?You're going to stay in a four-star hotel.
Who is going to travel with them?They're going to travel with friends.
How long are they going to stay?They're going to stay for a month.

💡 Structure

Wh-word + am/is/are + subject + going to + base verb + ?

🔑 كلمات الاستفهام: What – When – Which – How – Where – Who – How long

🎨 Position of Adjectives

Two Positions for Adjectives

الصفة في اللغة الإنجليزية لها موضعان في الجملة.

1️⃣ Before a noun (قبل الاسم)

Antarctica is an exotic place.

→ Adjective comes before the noun it describes.

2️⃣ After the verb be (بعد فعل الكون)

Antarctica is exotic.

→ Adjective comes after the verb be.

📝 Summary
🚀
Be Going To (+): I am going to travel. / She is going to leave.
🚫
Be Going To (–): He is not going to visit. / We are not going to go by plane.
❓
Yes/No Question: Are you going to travel? → Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
🔍
Wh- Questions: What/When/Where/How/Who/Which/How long + is/are + subject + going to + verb?
🎨
Adjectives position: Before noun → an exotic place. After be → The place is exotic.

✅ تذكّر: be going to تُستخدم للحديث عن خطط وعزم المستقبل.
✅ الصفة تأتي قبل الاسم أو بعد فعل الكون be.
