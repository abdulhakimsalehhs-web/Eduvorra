📚 Grammar: The Passive Voice

Why use the Passive? — لماذا نستخدم المبني للمجهول؟

Use the passive to emphasize the action, not the person or thing doing it.
نستخدمه للتركيز على الحدث وليس الفاعل.

Simple Present Passive: Subject + am/is/are + past participle
Simple Past Passive: Subject + was/were + past participle
With agent (by): ... + by + agent
Active VoicePassive Voice
Carbon makes diamonds. Diamonds are made entirely of carbon.
They found the Cullinan in 1905. The Cullinan was found in 1905.
Volcanic activity pushes diamonds up. Diamonds are pushed to the surface by volcanic activity.
An Italian couple invented the pencil. The pencil was invented by an Italian couple.

📝 Writing Corner Tips

Rule 1 – Use Passive to Focus on the Action

Use the passive to emphasize the action and not who or what does it.
نستخدم المبني للمجهول للتركيز على الحدث وليس على الفاعل.

Rule 2 – Form the Passive

Use the verb be + a past participle.

Simple present: Diamonds are made entirely of carbon.
Simple past: The Cullinan diamond was found in Africa.

Rule 3 – Use by for the Agent

Use by to show the person or thing that does the action.
نستخدم by لذكر من قام بالفعل.

Diamonds are pushed to the surface by volcanic activity.
The pencil was invented by an Italian couple named Bernacotti.

✍️ Exercise B – Writing Task

Instructions: Write about a place, a person, a thing, or an animal from a book of records. Say why it is the biggest, the best, the most wonderful, etc. Use the passive where possible.

اكتب عن مكان أو شخص أو شيء أو حيوان من سجل الأرقام القياسية. استخدم المبني للمجهول حيثما أمكن.

💡 Sample Answer – Model Paragraph
The Great Wall of China is the longest wall in the world. It was built over many centuries to protect China from invasions. It is considered one of the greatest wonders of the world. Today, it is visited by millions of tourists every year. The wall is made of stone, brick, and earth. It was listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1987.

📋 Lesson Summary
  • 🔵The Passive Voice emphasizes the action, not the doer.
  • 🔵Simple Present Passive: am/is/are + past participle
  • 🔵Simple Past Passive: was/were + past participle
  • 🔵Use by to mention the agent (the doer).
  • 🔵Diamonds are made of carbon → present passive.
  • 🔵The Cullinan was found in 1905 → past passive.
  • 🔵The nanodiamond is produced by compression of graphite.
📌 ملخص بالعربي
✅ المبني للمجهول = نركّز على الفعل لا الفاعل
✅ المضارع: am/is/are + past participle
✅ الماضي: was/were + past participle
✅ نستخدم by لذكر من قام بالفعل
