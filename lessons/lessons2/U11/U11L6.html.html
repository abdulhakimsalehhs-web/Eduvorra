📖 Grammar Explanation
💡
What do So and Such do?

So and such make the meaning of an adjective or adverb stronger.
So...that and such...that are used to show cause and effect.

يُستخدمان لتقوية المعنى وإظهار علاقة السبب والنتيجة — المبالغة في وصف شيء أدّى إلى نتيجة ما.

Formula / الصيغة Example / مثال
so + adjective/adverb + that He is so fast that he won the race.
He ran so quickly that he won the race.
so + many + plural noun + that He has so many books that he can hardly carry them.
so + much + noncount noun + that I have so much homework that I can't go out tonight.
such + adjective + noun + that It was such a difficult test that none of the students did well.
Ali is such a smart boy that he has the best grades.

✏️ Exercise A – So or Such?
A
Complete the sentences with so or such.

أكمل الجمل بـ so أو such

✅ ANSWER KEY – Exercise A
  1. Diamonds are so hard that you can't break them with a hammer.
  2. Sharks have such sensitive hearing that they can hear their prey miles away.
  3. It is such a luxurious hotel that the cheapest suite is $1,000 per night.
  4. He came into the room so quietly that no one heard him.
  5. The leather shoes were such a bargain that she bought three pairs.
  6. Ahmed was so hungry that he ate three burgers and two sides of fries.
✏️ Exercise B – So Many or So Much?
B
Complete the sentences with so many or so much.

أكمل الجمل بـ so many أو so much

✅ ANSWER KEY – Exercise B
  1. Our neighbors made so much noise that I couldn't sleep last night.
  2. There was so much traffic on the road that we arrived late.
  3. I have so many books that they don't all fit in my bookcase.
  4. He spends so much time working that he rarely sees his friends.
  5. There are so many species of animals that it's impossible to count them all.
✏️ Exercise C – Combine Sentences
C
Combine the sentences with so...that or such...that.

ادمج الجملتين باستخدام so...that أو such...that

✅ MODEL ANSWERS – Exercise C
  1. The children were so tired after their school trip that they fell asleep on the bus.
  2. It was such a beautiful day that we decided to go for a drive in the countryside.
  3. There were so many people in the supermarket that we had to wait in line for half an hour.
  4. The World Cup is such a popular sporting event that over a billion viewers watch it on TV.
  5. The Arabian Oryx was such an endangered species that it was extinct in the wild.

📋 Lesson Summary
📌
Key Points to Remember
  • ⚡so + adjective/adverb + that → He is so fast that he won.
  • 📚so many + plural noun + that → She has so many books that...
  • 💧so much + noncount noun + that → I have so much time that...
  • 🌟such + (a/an) + adj + noun + that → It was such a hot day that...
  • 🔗Both structures show cause and effect — the result follows from the strength of the description.
  • ✅Use so before adjectives/adverbs, and such before noun phrases.
